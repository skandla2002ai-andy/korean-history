Battle of Bongodong — Hong Beom-do's First Victory

In June 1920, Hong Beom-do's independence army crushed Japanese forces at Bongodong — the independence movement's first major military victory.

Era Japanese Colonial Period Topic War Topic Independence Movement Topic People Related Japan

Overview

The Battle of Bongodong (鳳梧洞 戰鬪) was fought on June 7, 1920, in the Bongodong valley of the Gando (Jiandao) region of Manchuria. A combined force of independence army units — including the Korean Independence Army led by Hong Beom-do (洪範圖) and the Military Governorate led by Choe Jin-dong — encircled and destroyed a Japanese cross-river pursuit force. Recorded as the first time the independence army defeated Japanese forces in a large-scale conventional engagement, it became, alongside the Battle of Cheongsanri, a symbol of the armed independence movement.

Background

Following the March First Movement (1919), the will for independence surged, and armed independence forces grew rapidly in the Gando region of Manchuria. Hong Beom-do organized the Korean Independence Army and repeatedly crossed the Tumen River to launch raids into the Korean interior. The Japanese military assembled a cross-river pursuit force and entered Gando in response to the independence army's guerrilla operations inside Korea.

Course of Battle

On June 4, 1920, after Japanese forces crossed the Tumen River and clashed with independence fighters at Samdunja, Hong Beom-do lured the enemy into the Bongodong valley. On June 7, the independence army mounted an ambush using the terrain to encircle the Japanese pursuit force. The Japanese suffered approximately 157 killed and over 300 wounded, while independence army casualties were minimal. This victory demonstrated the viability of armed resistance and greatly boosted Korean morale.

Historical Significance

The Battle of Bongodong holds great significance in the history of the independence movement as the first large-scale victory of independence fighters over a modern regular army. While the victory triggered Japan's large-scale dispatch of forces to Gando and the Gando Massacre (庚申慘變) of 1920, it also brought the independence army's military capabilities to international attention. General Hong Beom-do continued the independence movement and participated in the Battle of Cheongsanri; his remains were repatriated to Korea in 2021.
